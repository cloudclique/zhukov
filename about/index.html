Creative Roadmap

My Working Process

A comprehensive walkthrough of how every photoshoot is planned, directed, and crafted into final art.

1 Ideation

Picking a theme

Every shoot starts with a narrative foundation. We explore creative themes, character archetypes, mood boards, and evocative storytelling directions to set the project's aesthetic anchor.

3 Scouting

Location

We scout natural, urban, and studio locations with optimal lighting angles and background textures. Sun positions, shadow play, and spatial depth are mapped out beforehand.

5 Production
Live Capture Phase

Photoshoot

Production day is where vision meets spontaneous magic. I direct comfortable, natural posing, shape lighting with precision, and capture authentic expressions.

6 Post-Production

Editing

Careful image culling is followed by nuanced RAW processing, skin retouching, color grading curves, and tonal adjustments that elevate every frame.

Color Palette

Color harmony defines the visual mood. Using balanced chromatic palettes, we coordinate wardrobe tones, environment hues, gels, and ambient lights to produce a unified cinematic look.

4 Visual Blueprint

Moodboard

All concepts, poses, wardrobe suggestions, and lighting diagrams are assembled into a coherent moodboard. This gives everyone on set a clear vision and shared inspiration.

7 Completion

Finished, full-resolution files are delivered through a private, password-protected online client gallery. Includes web-optimized versions and master print-ready files.
